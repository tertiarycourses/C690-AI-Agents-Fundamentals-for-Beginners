## HOURS-01 - Opening hours

BrightDesk Workspace is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 am to 6:00 pm, excluding Singapore public holidays. Weekend access is not offered in this demonstration.

## PRICE-01 - Meeting room

The synthetic meeting-room rate is S$40 per hour with a minimum booking duration of two hours. A calculation is a non-binding estimate only. The FAQ does not contain live availability, discounts, taxes, deposits, or a booking service.

## PRICE-02 - Day pass

The synthetic day-pass rate is S$35 per person for one weekday. A day pass does not include a meeting room. Staff must confirm availability before a visitor travels to the workspace.

## PRICE-03 - Training room

The synthetic training-room rate is S$180 for a half day of up to four hours or S$320 for a full day of up to eight hours. Staff must confirm the intended date, setup, capacity, and availability before any booking.
